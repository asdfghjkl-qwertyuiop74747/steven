Science Fair 2026: Students Show Amazing Projects

By Daily News Hub Staff

This year's school science fair was held on Friday, and it was the biggest one yet. More than 50 students showed off projects about robots, volcanoes, plants, and even homemade rockets.

The judges were impressed by the creativity and hard work. "The projects this year were truly amazing," said one judge. "Students spent months preparing, and it really showed."

The first place prize went to a student who built a robot that can sort recycling automatically. The robot uses a simple camera and a moving arm to separate paper, plastic, and metal.

The fair will be open to parents and the public again next Friday afternoon in the school gym. Everyone is welcome to come and see what the students created.
